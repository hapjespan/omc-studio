# 0001: Lay out the canvas automatically, without a free drawing editor

Date: 2026-11-18
Status: superseded by 0013

## Decision

The canvas is laid out automatically from its structure: Domains flow as cards inside their Area, and the only layout setting per Domain is its width in columns (1 to 4). There is no free positioning, sizing, layering or guides.

## Motivation

The free editor was the most expensive part of the reference application, and automatic layout makes "create and assign it yourself" usable at once. Rejected: a free drawing editor. Settled by kickoff choice K1 and the "Not in version 1" list.
